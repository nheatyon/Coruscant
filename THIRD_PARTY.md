The original font and Earth-image notices remain with their public assets. The approved stack pins are in package.json and this project’s own lockfile; the specification documents the existing dependencies. This addition introduces one runtime package and one temporary verification package.

All application code, synthetic demonstration scenes and new test doubles are authored within this project. Generated provider-response doubles exist only in isolated tests; they are not acquired orbital records. Actual acquisition and numerical evidence are separately identified by fresh source hashes in the checklist. Existing fictional records, held-D0 tracks and supplied synthetic results retain their original labels and provenance. A real catalog identity does not imply taskability, booking, operator agreement or a real collection.
