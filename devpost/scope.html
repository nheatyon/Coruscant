PROOF / 01

Evidence changes discovery

Documented match, genuine known mismatch, and unknown-evidence case. Identity/operator, sensor/resolution, source/check date, match reasons and unresolved facts stay visible. “Documented criteria only” changes results; confirmed requirements and shortlist evidence reach the saved brief.

PROOF / 02

One durable lineage

The request/reference stays consistent across customer tracking, staff operations, Mission Control and the linked result after return/reload. Pre-acceptance edits/replies obey authority; accepted work remains fixed.

PROOF / 03

Basic failure correctness — Now

Repeating the same submission or decision after an uncertain outcome does not create duplicate records or apply the decision twice. Refuse a stale request or superseded/expired quote decision. Failed actions preserve unsent input. Richer merge/retry/conflict-recovery interfaces remain Later; exact mechanisms belong in 4-spec.

PROOF / 04

Authority is enforced

Show permitted acceptance; refused Requester and Viewer decisions; refused cross-organization record/private-file reads; and refused customer staff actions. Hiding controls alone does not prove permission enforcement.

PROOF / 05

Two independent gates

Publication and entitlement are separately demonstrable. Neither alone exposes unpublished or unentitled material. Visibility requires current membership in the matching request/result organization, valid permitted-use rights, active entitlement, and published material. Organization B cannot obtain A's private records by knowing their identifiers.

PROOF / 06

Explainable comparison

Two visibly different, dated baseline/recent images carry source/provider/sensor and permitted-use metadata. Zoom/pan, comparison, a saved point note and a staff-authored, staff-reviewed finding connect the evidence to the question and its uncertainty.

PROOF / 07

Claims stay bounded

Technical relevance establishes no area/time coverage, availability, price or booking. The compelling moment is a connected authorized decision and explainable visual comparison whose records remain on return.

The Unique Kernel

StarSearch discovery, Mission Control decisions, and SatSuite understanding share the same saved mission records. Ordinary StarSearch uses the full adopted CelesTrak SAR+Earth Resources real-catalog source scope and its bounded predicted orbital context. A disclosed submitted search can propose a sourced place from the locally extracted phrase. After deliberate application/independent circle confirmation and reviewed requirements/window, automatic technical planning combines sampled center-point LOS with documented compatibility and the approved nominal-center/illumination checks. The customer inspects tentative options and explicitly saves the same request as Planning before separately requesting staff review. These bounded proposals are not whole-area imageability or operator reservations. The original SAR comparison journey continues through a staff quote, authorized acceptance, recorded schedule, and entitled supplied result, then returns to that exact result and saved observation.

The Core Loop

Describe the EO need → explicitly explore matches and, when disclosed, find the named place → inspect/correct/apply and separately confirm the sourced or manual Earth circle → supply genuinely missing requirements, observation intent and independent planning window → automatically inspect technical options and tentative scheduling → explicitly save the chosen plan in the same request as Planning, or save reviewed preparation with No selected plan → separately submit that request for staff review when desired → staff publishes a quote → an authorized customer accepts → staff records a schedule → staff prepares/reviews and publishes compatible supplied material, separately grants entitlement, and records delivery → the customer compares that mission's result in SatSuite and saves a point observation.

Customers return to track decisions, respond to clarifications, and reopen the result with its saved observation. Staff and customers act on the same persisted request, quote, schedule, and result; a display clock never advances workflow status.
