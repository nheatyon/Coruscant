<a id="scope-kernel"></a>
## The Unique Kernel

StarSearch discovery, Mission Control decisions, and SatSuite understanding share the same saved mission records. Ordinary StarSearch uses the full adopted CelesTrak SAR+Earth Resources real-catalog source scope and its bounded predicted orbital context. A disclosed submitted search can propose a sourced place from the locally extracted phrase. After deliberate application/independent circle confirmation and reviewed requirements/window, automatic technical planning combines sampled center-point LOS with documented compatibility and the approved nominal-center/illumination checks. The customer inspects tentative options and explicitly saves the same request as Planning before separately requesting staff review. These bounded proposals are not whole-area imageability or operator reservations. The original SAR comparison journey continues through a staff quote, authorized acceptance, recorded schedule, and entitled supplied result, then returns to that exact result and saved observation.

## Who It's For

A small coastal/environmental research team asking: “Help our team compare possible surface-water expansion before and after heavy rainfall within this selected river/estuary area.” Their current procurement process has not been established; the stated need is to avoid requiring satellite procurement expertise.

<a id="scope-core-loop"></a>
## The Core Loop

Describe the EO need → explicitly explore matches and, when disclosed, find the named place → inspect/correct/apply and separately confirm the sourced or manual Earth circle → supply genuinely missing requirements, observation intent and independent planning window → automatically inspect technical options and tentative scheduling → explicitly save the chosen plan in the same request as Planning, or save reviewed preparation with No selected plan → separately submit that request for staff review when desired → staff publishes a quote → an authorized customer accepts → staff records a schedule → staff prepares/reviews and publishes compatible supplied material, separately grants entitlement, and records delivery → the customer compares that mission's result in SatSuite and saves a point observation.

Customers return to track decisions, respond to clarifications, and reopen the result with its saved observation. Staff and customers act on the same persisted request, quote, schedule, and result; a display clock never advances workflow status.

<a id="scope-working-proof"></a>
## What “Working” Looks Like

One representative persisted Earth-observation journey works from public discovery through customer result inspection. The demonstration brief uses SAR with a requested maximum documented ground resolution of **10 m**, one explicitly confirmed river/estuary circle (25 km radius is an example to confirm), a clear observation window, and distinct baseline/recent dates. This is a demonstration requirement, not a universal scientific threshold or a guarantee of answerability.

The added planning proof also carries a genuinely sourced non-SAR example through the same confirmed area, single-observation or comparison intent, automatic bounded options and explicit Planning save. It preserves band/mode/native-resolution facts and unknowns. Planning is useful without optical fulfillment: the synthetic SAR pair is never relabeled as optical or imagery of a newly geocoded place. Exact proposed collection timing is a checked nominal instant where supported; broad LOS intervals remain provisional context, and delivery timing is Not established without a cited provider fact or explicit processing assumption.

The proof includes:

- A documented candidate match, a genuine known mismatch, and an unknown-evidence case. Show identity/operator, documented sensor/resolution where known, source/check date, match reasons, and unresolved facts. “Documented criteria only” actually changes results. Confirmed requirements and shortlist evidence survive into the saved brief. Technical relevance does not establish area/time coverage, availability, price, or booking.
- A saved request and reference that remain consistent across customer tracking, staff operations, Mission Control, and the linked SatSuite result after returning/reloading. Pre-acceptance edits/replies work within authority; accepted work stays fixed.
- Basic failure correctness: repeating the same submission or decision after an uncertain outcome does not create duplicate records or apply the decision twice; a stale request or superseded/expired quote decision is refused; a failed action preserves the user's unsent input. Richer merge/retry/conflict-recovery interfaces remain Later; exact mechanisms belong in `4-spec`.
- A permitted quote acceptance, refused Requester and Viewer decisions, refused cross-organization reads including private image/file access, and refused customer attempts at staff actions. These are enforced decisions, not merely hidden controls.
- Separately demonstrable publication and entitlement gates: neither alone exposes unpublished or unentitled material. Visibility requires current membership in the matching request/result organization, valid permitted-use rights, active entitlement, and published material. Organization B cannot obtain A's private records by knowing identifiers.
- Two visibly different, dated baseline/recent supplied images with source/provider/sensor and permitted-use metadata; zoom/pan and comparison; at least one persisted point observation/note; and a staff-authored, staff-reviewed finding citing supplied evidence and explaining uncertainty. Returning to the mission reopens that exact result and observation.

The compelling moment is following the same question from candidate evidence to an authorized decision and then to an explainable visual comparison, with the linked records still present on return.
