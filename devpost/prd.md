## The Core Journey — UI-01 / DISC-01 / BRIEF-02 / MISSION-02 / SAT-01

Source: [scope — The Unique Kernel](scope.md#scope-kernel), [The Core Loop](scope.md#scope-core-loop), [What “Working” Looks Like](scope.md#scope-working-proof).

1. A visitor opens public StarSearch without signing in. They describe the research need, review interpreted requirements and inspect the synchronized list/globe. Real catalog is the ordinary source, with exact scope/version and paused predicted UTC; separate Demonstration mode retains its fictional labels/held D0. No displayed track asserts actual telemetry, coverage or availability. All four service rows remain visible; a deferred-family choice or clearly non-EO need opens its honest Planned informational boundary and retains prior EO work.
2. They inspect candidate evidence, distinguish documented criteria from unknown facts, and optionally shortlist candidates. Their original question, selected evidence and reviewed criteria remain connected. A documented mismatch is excluded from matches; unknown evidence is visibly unverified and excluded when documented-only filtering is enabled.
3. They prepare the same EO brief with **Single observation / Baseline–recent comparison**, SAR/Visible/SWIR/Thermal requirements and actual band/mode evidence. The disclosed submitted search may find a locally extracted place phrase; the sourced proposed center/radius carries directly into this brief without coordinate re-entry, then requires deliberate application and separate area-size confirmation. No new question silently replaces a confirmed target. Comparison dates start blank and remain independent calendar dates; they are required and ordered before comparison fulfillment submission, while Single observation invents no pair dates and unfinished Planning preparation may retain named missing fields. A valid explicit future planning window and ready inputs automatically produce bounded LOS/nominal-center/sunlight options and tentative scheduling without staff entry. A checked proposed instant is not a booked mission or full-area imageability. The preserved complete SAR/Imagery comparison remains the end-to-end supplied-result demonstration; optical/single-observation fulfillment and Video/GIS remain unsupported.
4. Before any durable first save, an anonymous visitor opens a permitted customer context. The same-tab brief/target/selected option survive; entry itself saves or submits nothing. Final review names the actual destination, even with one workspace. Owner/Requester explicitly chooses **Save planning request** without an option or **Save proposed plan** with the exact option; Viewer cannot write. These create **Planning**, not a staff review task. **Submit request for review** separately validates the completed intent-specific brief/contact and moves that same reference to Submitted.
5. A saved receipt gives one immutable request reference. Customer Requests/Mission Control and authorized staff detail read the same Planning or submitted record, brief/plan versions and evidence after return/reload/restart. Planning says **Review not requested** and is excluded from the staff review queue. Only explicit submission makes it a review task; later staff clarification/replies remain tied to the same request. Changing real brief inputs revises the brief and stales affected unaccepted offers; choosing another option for unchanged inputs adds only a plan version.
6. Staff Admin composes a private quote against the current reviewed brief, including both requested comparison dates and a resolved request window for the complete demonstration. **Save draft** is private. Explicit review and **Confirm publish** make one current offer customer-visible and put the request in **Quote ready**. Another private draft does not withdraw a still-current published offer.
7. The Owner with current organization-specific offer-selection authority reviews the published terms and explicitly accepts. Acceptance records intent, actor and time; it is not payment, operator confirmation or a tasking command. The request becomes **Accepted** only after success. A decline instead returns it to **In review**, preserving the reason and offer history.
8. Staff records the initial schedule against immutable accepted terms after a separate review/confirmation. Customer views show the same provider, display-zone/UTC window, optional tracked demonstration objects, notes and schedule version. Clock expiry changes countdown wording, never delivery status.
9. Staff prepares the supplied demonstration result for the same accepted request: a visibly distinct synthetic Baseline/Recent pair, metadata/rights, and a basic evidence-cited finding. Check the supplied illustrative dates against the agreed comparison context without silently substituting them for requested dates. Actual collection is **Not collected — synthetic demonstration**; illustrative dates never masquerade as acquisition times.
10. Staff explicitly reviews and publishes material. Review is separate from saving; publication is separate from organization entitlement. A finding cannot publish while its cited image versions are unpublished. A separate reviewed access change grants the intended organization only the permitted access.
11. Staff records a new delivery only when the intended customer can read the complete required published pair and reviewed/published finding under valid rights and entitlement, and each supplied illustrative date equals its corresponding accepted Baseline/Recent reference date. A mismatch blocks the new action with **Delivery blocked: supplied comparison dates differ from the accepted reference dates.** and keeps the request Scheduled. Review acknowledgement cannot waive this condition. Neither publication, entitlement nor time passing records delivery by itself.
12. The customer follows the exact mission result link into SatSuite, reads the initially selected **Staff finding** tab while comparing the labeled images, and may add a point note through **Your observations**. On returning through the mission, the same result, versions and actual saved observation remain available while authorized.

## Shared Failure and Return Expectations — SR-01 / SR-02 / OPS-01

Source: [scope — The POC Boundary — Approved Now, Basic failure correctness](scope.md#scope-now).

- A named validation error appears beside the affected input and in accessible feedback; raw invalid values and unsent work remain editable. Blank optional facts are unknown, not zero or fabricated completeness.
- An unchanged retry after an uncertain submission/quote decision/publication/schedule/delivery/note outcome must not create another record or apply the same action twice. Describe whether the action was saved or remains uncertain; a failed detail refresh after success does not invite a second action.
- A stale request, changed brief, superseded/expired quote or obsolete confirmation is refused. Retain entered terms/reason/note and require review of the current record before another attempt. Rich merge/retry/conflict-recovery interfaces are Later; exact mechanisms are for `4-spec`.
- Changing organization or customer/staff context invalidates open decision confirmations. Unsent private work remains bound to its original customer/workspace/request; it is never silently transferred or replayed in another context.
- Before leaving an edited private form, **Stay here / Keep draft and continue** makes the choice explicit. This retains work, not a submission, publication, grant or ownership transfer. Optional durable device draft saving retains the packet's disclosure/consent boundary; saved request persistence is not blanket consent to persist private drafts or imagery.
- Returning to a private tab covers retained content while checking access. Temporary failure shows **Check account / Try again**, retaining unsent work behind the cover. Actual access loss or a different identity hides prior drafts, receipts, images and result metadata; late responses cannot reveal old-context content. Browser Back after exit must not expose it.
- Successful empty, loading, operational failure, unavailable provider and permission denial are distinguishable. A failed catalog load is not zero matches; a failed result fetch is not no result; a schedule-detail failure is not proof no plan exists.
- Returning/reloading preserves the actual saved request/reference, revisions, current published/accepted quote, schedule, result versions, staff review/publication/access state and saved point notes. Access checks apply again; persistence never overrides current permission.
